01 — About

A one-person signal box

I'm a diagram maker obsessed with how a real lever frame reads. Every route, every locking lever and every point gets its own line, the way it would on a printed board in a working box.

It's a small personal studio — nothing corporate about it. Don't be shy, I don't bite. The more detail you give me, the better the board turns out.
